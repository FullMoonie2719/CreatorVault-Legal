# CreatorVault Account Deletion

Last updated: 8 October 2026

## Delete your account in the app

If you can still sign in to CreatorVault:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Open **Your data**.
3. Choose **Permanently delete account**.
4. Type the requested confirmation and re-enter your current password.
5. Complete the deletion request.

CreatorVault's authenticated deletion flow is designed to remove the account login,
associated private media, and associated CreatorVault account records.

Export anything you need before deleting the account. Permanent deletion cannot be
undone.

## Request deletion without the app

If CreatorVault is uninstalled or you cannot use the in-app deletion screen, send an
account-deletion request to **Tracksuitchav@proton.me**.

To help verify account ownership without receiving sensitive media:

- contact us using the email address associated with the CreatorVault account where
  possible;
- state that you are requesting permanent deletion of your CreatorVault account;
- do **not** attach private photos or videos.

We may ask for reasonable account-ownership verification before performing a
destructive deletion. We will never ask you to email your CreatorVault password.

## What deletion covers

A completed account deletion is designed to remove:

- the CreatorVault authentication account;
- private uploaded media and stored thumbnails;
- account/profile records;
- creator metadata, collections, tags, review state, processing records, AI draft
  suggestions, and associated account data.

If any information must be retained for a legal, security, fraud-prevention, or
regulatory reason, the published privacy policy must explain the applicable
retention.

## Individual media deletion

Deleting the full account is not required to remove one item. CreatorVault also has
a permanent per-media deletion action inside the private Vault.
